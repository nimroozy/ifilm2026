# Collections V1

Draft scaffold for curated content collections.

## Scope (this phase)

- Curated collections of movies and series
- Admin create/edit of collection metadata and membership
- Customer browse surface for published collections
- Deterministic ordering

## Out of scope (do not mix)

- Watchlist
- Continue Watching V2
- Request Movie
- What to Watch
- Recommendations
- CDN / Live TV
- Local multi-track packaging

## Status

Scaffold only — implementation follows after v1.6.0 production confirmation.
